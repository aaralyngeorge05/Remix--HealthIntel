# User Guide & Capstone Demonstration Manual

## AI-Powered Health Information Intelligence & Credibility Assessment System

---

## 1. Quick Demonstration Workflow

The application includes pre-loaded live scenarios designed for rapid evaluation:

### Demo 1: Health Misinformation Debunking
1. Navigate to **Health Claim Checker**.
2. Click **Demo 1: "Vitamin C cures a cold in one day."** or enter any custom health statement.
3. Click **Assess Credibility**.
4. Observe the 8-stage explainable pipeline animation, the **Partially Supported** verdict, AI confidence %, separate Evidence Strength badge, explanation, and peer-reviewed Cochrane Systematic Review reference.

### Demo 2 & 3: Cross-Document Discrepancy & Allergy Conflict
1. Switch role to **Caregiver** or **Healthcare Professional** using the top header.
2. Navigate to **Patient & Inconsistency View**.
3. Select **Robert Miller (MRN: RM-948210)**.
4. Click **Cross-Doc Inconsistencies**:
   - **Discrepancy 1**: Metformin 500 mg (Outpatient Prescription) vs Metformin 1000 mg (Hospital Discharge Summary).
   - **Discrepancy 2**: Documented Penicillin allergy vs prescribed Amoxicillin-Clavulanate.
5. Click **Questions for Healthcare Provider** to inspect synthesized doctor visit questions.
6. Click **Print for Doctor Visit** to prepare questions for a clinical consultation.

### Demo 4: Dangerous Claim Interception
1. Return to **Health Claim Checker**.
2. Click **Demo 3: "Stop insulin and use cinnamon instead."**
3. Click **Assess Credibility**.
4. Observe the immediate **Potentially Dangerous** verdict, Critical Risk badge, and the emergency red banner warning against stopping prescribed diabetes therapy.

## 2. Document Upload & Processing
1. Navigate to **Document Upload & OCR**.
2. Select target patient (e.g., Robert Miller) and document category (e.g., Prescription, Discharge Summary, Lab Report).
3. Drag and drop any PDF, image (PNG/JPG), or TXT file into the dropzone.
4. Click **Extract & Reconcile Information**.
5. The extracted medications, dosages, allergies, and lab results are immediately reconciled into the patient's longitudinal profile.
